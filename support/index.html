Support

We're here to help. Email hershapplications@gmail.com or tap Send Feedback in the app's Account tab.

Do I need an account?

No. Bible Out Loud uses the Apple ID your iPhone or iPad is signed into. If the app asks you to sign in to iCloud, open Settings, tap Sign In at the top, then return to the app.

Where are my recordings saved?

On your device first, then backed up to your own iCloud account. They count toward your iCloud storage.

How do I share my Bible?

Tap Share My Bible in the Library tab, enter a friend's email address or phone number, and send them the invite. Once they tap it, your Bible shows up in their Library. They'll need the free Bible Out Loud app on an iPhone or iPad.

Is sharing free?

Sharing with your first friend is free. A one-time purchase unlocks sharing with as many people as you like, and it works for your whole family with Family Sharing. Listening to Bibles shared with you is always free.

I already bought Unlock Sharing. How do I get it back?

Open Share My Bible and tap Restore Purchase, while signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I stop sharing with someone?

In Share My Bible, swipe left on their name and tap Remove. Their access ends right away. Recordings they already downloaded may stay on their device.

Can I read from my own printed Bible?

Yes. Choose Own Bible at the top of the Read tab to hide the on-screen text and show just the verse reference.

How do I delete a recording, or all my data?

Delete one recording with the trash button in the Read tab or the ••• menu in Library. Delete Account in the Account tab permanently deletes everything you've recorded and shared, from your device and from iCloud. Your Apple ID isn't affected.
