Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

What we collect

Bible Out Loud doesn't have its own accounts or passwords. It uses the Apple ID your iPhone or iPad is signed into, and we never see your email address or password. You can add your name and, if you like, the Bible version you usually read. When you record a verse, the app saves the audio recording of your voice.

Where your data lives

Your recordings are saved on your device first, then backed up to your own iCloud account, which is run by Apple. They count toward your iCloud storage, and we can't see or listen to them.

Your recordings are private

Every recording you make is private by default and visible only to you. If you choose Share My Bible and send someone an invite, they can listen to everything you've recorded and download it for offline listening, but they can never change it. You can stop sharing with anyone at any time, though recordings a friend has already downloaded may remain on their device. We do not share your recordings with anyone else or any third party.

How we use your information

Your name is shown to friends you share your Bible with, and when you accept a friend's invite, your name is shared with them so they can see who's listening. If you choose a preferred Bible version, an anonymous copy of just the version name (not your name or Apple ID) is saved where we can see it, so we can decide which versions to add to the app. If you send us feedback, we receive your email address and the app and iOS version you're using, so we can help.

What we don't do

We don't show ads, sell your data, or share it with advertisers or data brokers. We don't use your recordings for any purpose other than playing them back to you and the friends you've shared with.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual recordings at any time. Delete Account in the Account tab permanently deletes everything you've recorded and shared in Bible Out Loud, from your device and from iCloud. It does not affect your Apple ID.
